gecko's abilities

being a hybrid, diego shares some abilities with actual geckos. he can scale tall buildings and even climb on the ceiling thanks to his adhesive hands and feet, and he possess a tail which can actually be detached and regrown as a defence mechanism like actual geckos- this trait also carries over to certain limbs. there are some drawbacks, being that he is sensetive to temperature as most cold blooded animals are, and due to most reptiles having to shed their skin, he functionally has eczema and senstive skin.
