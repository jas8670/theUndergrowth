the city

the city of the undergrowth consists of several districts that house higher concentrations of certain species, all controlled by respective powerful gangs. an example would be the reptile district, where gecko currently resides. it's been most recently ruled over by the viper gang, a group of powerful snake hybrids who currently own a majority of the cities bars, clubs and other recreational facilities as fronts for their operations.
